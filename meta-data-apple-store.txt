APP NAME
Kilnslip

SUBTITLE
A fired practice, offline

KEYWORDS
kiln,breath,walk,journal,offline,focus,practice,private,teacher,columns,firing

PROMOTION
An on-device teacher: four walk steps, a rising-column breath, and a private journal.

DESCRIPTION
Kilnslip is a complete offline teacher for a short practice you keep on the phone.

Begin with Walk: four prompts named Set, Aim, Cross, and Hold. Open Four Columns and let one form fill from the base through inhale, hold, exhale, and rest. Set the firing length in Range, then press what you noticed into a Tablet note.

WHAT YOU GET

• Onboarding that introduces the whole lesson
• Walk, four guidance steps you advance one at a time
• Four Columns, a timed rising-column breath
• Range, a session window of 8, 16, 22, or 40 minutes
• Tablet, a private journal saved on the device

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

There is no account, no sign-in, no cloud sync, and no remote AI. Kilnslip makes no network calls. Your range and Tablet notes stay in local storage on your phone.
