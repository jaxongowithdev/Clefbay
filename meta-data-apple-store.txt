APP NAME
Fernquest

SUBTITLE
Word hunt, fully offline

KEYWORDS
game,word,hunt,letters,grid,quest,offline,puzzle,find,glade,notes

PROMOTION
An on-device word hunt. Trace letters across the wooden grid and keep a private field book.

DESCRIPTION
Fernquest is a complete offline word hunt in a green glade.

The home board is a wooden letter grid. Tap letters from left to right to find PATH, FIND, LORE, and QUEST. A finished word turns green and scores. Begin with Walk: four moves named Scan, Trace, Mark, and Hunt. Open Four Columns and fill four letter rows. Choose the trail clock in Range, then write which word you found in a Tablet note.

WHAT YOU GET

• Onboarding that opens the glade
• A playable word grid with a target list
• Walk, four moves you advance one at a time
• Four Columns, a timed letter-row drill
• Range, a trail clock of 5, 9, 14, or 22 minutes
• Tablet, a private journal saved on the device
• A tap sound when a word is found

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

There is no account, no sign-in, no cloud sync, and no remote AI. Fernquest makes no network calls. Your range and Tablet notes stay in local storage on your phone.
