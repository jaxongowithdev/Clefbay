APP NAME
Plumline

SUBTITLE
A thread of practice, offline

KEYWORDS
thread,breath,walk,journal,offline,focus,practice,private,teacher,columns,minutes

PROMOTION
An on-device teacher: four walk steps, a rising-column breath, and a private journal.

DESCRIPTION
Plumline is a complete offline teacher for a short practice you keep on the phone.

Begin with Walk: four prompts named Anchor, Sweep, Pace, and Pin. Open Four Columns and let one bead rise through inhale, hold, exhale, and rest. Set the thread length in Range, then pin what you noticed as a Tablet note.

WHAT YOU GET

• Onboarding that introduces the whole lesson
• Walk, four guidance steps you advance one at a time
• Four Columns, a timed rising-column breath
• Range, a session window of 4, 9, 14, or 24 minutes
• Tablet, a private journal saved on the device

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

There is no account, no sign-in, no cloud sync, and no remote AI. Plumline makes no network calls. Your range and Tablet notes stay in local storage on your phone.
