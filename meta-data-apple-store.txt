APP NAME
Bandkit

SUBTITLE
Instrument games, fully offline

KEYWORDS
games,music,instrument,drum,bell,strings,offline,journal,play,teacher,band

PROMOTION
An on-device teacher for four instrument games, four wooden bars, and a private band book.

DESCRIPTION
Bandkit is a complete offline teacher for parlor instrument games.

Begin with Walk: four games named Pluck, Tap, Reed, and Shake. Open Four Columns and lift four wooden bars through cue, strike, ring, and cut. Choose the round length in Range, then write which game you played in a Tablet note.

WHAT YOU GET

• Onboarding that introduces the whole set of games
• Walk, four instrument games you advance one at a time
• Four Columns, a timed drill that lifts four wooden bars
• Range, a round of 3, 7, 11, or 21 minutes
• Tablet, a private journal saved on the device

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

There is no account, no sign-in, no cloud sync, and no remote AI. Bandkit makes no network calls. Your range and Tablet notes stay in local storage on your phone.
