APP NAME
Amberwick

SUBTITLE
Offline teacher, on device

KEYWORDS
ledger,breath,walk,journal,offline,focus,practice,private,minutes,teacher,columns

PROMOTION
A device-only teacher: four walk steps, a rising-column breath, and a private journal.

DESCRIPTION
Amberwick is a complete offline teacher for a short practice you file on the phone itself.

Begin with Walk: four prompts named Still, Frame, Travel, and Note. Open Four Columns and watch one column rise through inhale, hold, exhale, and rest. Set the minutes in Range, then file what you noticed as a Tablet note.

WHAT YOU GET

• Onboarding that introduces the whole lesson
• Walk, four guidance steps you advance one at a time
• Four Columns, a timed rising-column breath
• Range, a session window of 5, 10, 15, or 25 minutes
• Tablet, a private journal saved on the device

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

There is no account, no sign-in, no cloud sync, and no remote AI. Amberwick makes no network calls. Your range and Tablet notes stay in local storage on your phone.
