APP NAME
Cropmark

SUBTITLE
Sheet layout, fully offline

KEYWORDS
layout,print,columns,folio,offline,journal,pages,draft,teacher,sheet,notes

PROMOTION
An on-device teacher for marking a sheet, setting four columns, and keeping a private press log.

DESCRIPTION
Cropmark is a complete offline teacher for laying out one printed sheet.

Begin with Walk: four prompts named Head, Gutter, Foot, and Fore. Open Four Columns and raise four columns of type through set, lead, lock, and pull. Choose the press-run length in Range, then write what the sheet needed in a Tablet note.

WHAT YOU GET

• Onboarding that introduces the whole lesson
• Walk, four margin steps you advance one at a time
• Four Columns, a timed drill for setting four columns of type
• Range, a press run of 5, 15, 25, or 45 minutes
• Tablet, a private journal saved on the device

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

There is no account, no sign-in, no cloud sync, and no remote AI. Cropmark makes no network calls. Your range and Tablet notes stay in local storage on your phone.
