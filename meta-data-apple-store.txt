APP NAME
Saltpaper

SUBTITLE
A paper teacher, offline

KEYWORDS
ticket,breath,walk,journal,offline,focus,practice,private,teacher,columns,paper

PROMOTION
A paper teacher on your phone: four walk steps, a rising-column breath, and a private journal.

DESCRIPTION
Saltpaper is a complete offline teacher for a short practice you keep on the phone.

Begin with Walk: four prompts named Pause, Turn, Drift, and Land. Open Four Columns and let one window rise through inhale, hold, exhale, and rest. Set the ticket length in Range, then stamp what you noticed as a Tablet note.

WHAT YOU GET

• Onboarding that introduces the whole lesson
• Walk, four guidance steps you advance one at a time
• Four Columns, a timed rising-column breath
• Range, a session window of 6, 12, 18, or 30 minutes
• Tablet, a private journal saved on the device

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

There is no account, no sign-in, no cloud sync, and no remote AI. Saltpaper makes no network calls. Your range and Tablet notes stay in local storage on your phone.
