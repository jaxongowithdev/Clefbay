APP NAME
Orchard Bell

SUBTITLE
A quiet offline teacher

KEYWORDS
orchard,bell,attention,breathe,steps,notes,offline,practice,window,journal,teacher

PROMOTION
A pocket teacher for a four-step room walk, a rising-column breath, and a private journal.

DESCRIPTION
Orchard Bell is a complete offline teacher for a short practice you can do in the room you are already in.

Begin with Walk: four prompts named Settle, Look, Cross, and Choose. Open Four Columns and let one vessel rise at a time through inhale, hold, exhale, and rest. Set a session window in Range, then keep what you noticed in a Tablet note.

WHAT YOU GET

• Onboarding that introduces the whole lesson
• Walk, four guidance steps you advance one at a time
• Four Columns, a timed rising-column breath
• Range, a session window of 2, 6, 9, or 15 minutes
• Tablet, a private journal saved on the device

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

There is no account, no sign-in, no cloud sync, and no remote AI. Orchard Bell makes no network calls. Your range and Tablet notes stay in local storage on your phone.
