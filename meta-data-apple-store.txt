APP NAME
Notehall

SUBTITLE
Instrument tile match, fully offline

KEYWORDS
game,music,instrument,match,tiles,orchestra,offline,score,puzzle,hall,notes

PROMOTION
An on-device instrument tile game. Match the hall, collect the order, and keep a private score book.

DESCRIPTION
Notehall is a complete offline instrument tile game set in an orchestra hall.

The home board shows instrument tiles. Collect them in the order marked under the grid. Begin with Walk: four moves named Pick, Pair, Order, and Score. Open Four Columns and stack tiles through pick, pair, order, and score. Choose the hall clock in Range, then write which instrument you matched in a Tablet note.

WHAT YOU GET

• Onboarding that opens the hall
• A playable instrument tile board with a collect order
• Walk, four moves you advance one at a time
• Four Columns, a timed stack of tiles
• Range, a hall clock of 8, 12, 20, or 40 minutes
• Tablet, a private journal saved on the device

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

There is no account, no sign-in, no cloud sync, and no remote AI. Notehall makes no network calls. Your range and Tablet notes stay in local storage on your phone.
