APP NAME
Clefbay

SUBTITLE
Instrument bay, fully offline

KEYWORDS
game,music,instrument,violin,trumpet,map,shop,offline,melody,assemble

PROMOTION
An on-device instrument bay. Fit the horn, open the map, and keep a private field book.

DESCRIPTION
Clefbay is a complete offline instrument game in a teal bay.

Home opens six halls. Fit the horn by tapping Bell, Valve, Slide, and Mouth. A finished build turns gold, scores, and plays a tap sound. The map has String Forest, Brass Canyon, and a locked Reed bay. The shelf keeps eight instruments. The card replays the bay tune. The shop spends stars that stay on the phone. The hall board is a local list. Begin with Walk: four moves named Open, Tap, Score, and Next. Open Four Columns and run Fit, Tune, Check, and Keep. Choose the bay clock in Range, then write which part you fit in a Tablet note.

WHAT YOU GET

• Onboarding that opens the bay
• A playable horn build with a remembered tune
• A world map, shelf, shop, instrument card, and phone-only board
• Walk, four moves you advance one at a time
• Four Columns, a timed bay drill
• Range, a bay clock of 5, 9, 14, or 22 minutes
• Tablet, a private journal saved on the device
• A tap sound when a part locks in

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

There is no account, no sign-in, no cloud sync, and no remote AI. Clefbay makes no network calls. Your range, stars, and Tablet notes stay in local storage on your phone.
