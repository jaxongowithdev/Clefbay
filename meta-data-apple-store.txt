APP NAME
Morrow Steps

SUBTITLE
Private practice, fully offline

KEYWORDS
breathing,walk,focus,offline,journal,attention,practice,calm,private,reflection,reset

PROMOTION
A quiet practice teacher for walking, breathing, and private reflection — fully offline.

DESCRIPTION
Morrow Steps is a private practice teacher for small moments of attention.

Begin with Walk: four clear prompts to arrive, notice, move, and return. Open Four Columns for a rising-column breath that guides inhale, hold, exhale, and rest. Set a practice window in Range, then put down what you noticed in a Tablet note.

WHAT YOU GET

• A complete, gentle onboarding
• Walk, a four-step return-to-the-room practice
• Four Columns, a timed rising-column breath
• Range controls for a 3, 5, 7, or 10 minute practice window
• Tablet, a journal for notes saved directly on your device

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

There is no account, no sign-in, no cloud sync, and no remote AI. Morrow Steps makes no network calls. Your practice preferences and Tablet notes stay in local storage on your phone.
