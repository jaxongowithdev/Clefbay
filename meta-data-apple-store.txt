APP NAME
Sparglass

SUBTITLE
Offline practice teacher

KEYWORDS
teacher,breath,walking,journal,offline,focus,practice,private,session,local,columns

PROMOTION
An on-device teacher for a four-step walk, a rising-column breath, and a private journal.

DESCRIPTION
Sparglass is a complete offline teacher for a short attention practice.

Begin with Walk: four prompts named Plant, Count, Pace, and Mark. Open Four Columns for a rising-column breath — inhale, hold, exhale, rest. Set a session window in Range, then write what remains in a Tablet note.

WHAT YOU GET

• Onboarding that introduces the whole practice
• Walk, four guidance steps you advance one at a time
• Four Columns, a timed rising-column breath
• Range, a session window of 4, 8, 12, or 20 minutes
• Tablet, a private journal saved on the device

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

There is no account, no sign-in, no cloud sync, and no remote AI. Sparglass makes no network calls. Your range and Tablet notes stay in local storage on your phone.
